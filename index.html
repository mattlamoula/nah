The rules of GOOB

Simple. Every number is live from the engine. Every hunt leaves transactions on the explorer: claim, buy $STONK, feed.

  1. On the clock

    A hunt fires every 3 minutes. Always. Nobody can time it, everybody can see it coming.

  2. 100% of the fees

    Everything the hunt takes goes into $STONK. The team keeps nothing.

  3. One buy

    All the SOL (minus a tiny gas reserve) becomes one buy of $STONK.

  4. Fed, not burned

    GOOB does not buy itself. Supply stays 1B. Holders eat the bag it fetches.

  5. Small fees wait

    Under the minimum, it waits and joins the next hunt. A failed hunt retries on the next one.
